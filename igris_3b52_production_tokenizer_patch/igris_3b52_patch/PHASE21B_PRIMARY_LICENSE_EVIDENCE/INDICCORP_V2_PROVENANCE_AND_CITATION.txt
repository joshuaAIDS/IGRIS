Corpus Construction Methodology:
1. Web Scraping & Source Aggregation:
   - Aggregated text from news portals, magazines, blogs, and public web domains in 22 scheduled Indian languages.
   - Crawled national and regional news portals with high editorial standards to ensure native fluency.
   - Integrated Indic Wikipedia dumps and open public cultural archives.
2. Cleaning & Normalization Pipeline:
   - Indic NLP Library text cleaning: Unicode NFC normalization, script-specific zero-width character handling
     (ZWJ U+200D and ZWNJ U+200C retention for morphological validity).
   - Language identification filtering using fastText and Indic-specific CLD models (threshold > 0.85).
   - Document-level and sentence-level deduplication via MinHash and strict line hashing.
   - Structural punctuation normalization and HTML tag stripping.
3. Dataset Releases:
   - IndicCorp v1 (2020): Initial release covering 11 major languages, ~450 million tokens total.
   - IndicCorp v2 (2022): Substantially expanded release covering 22 languages, >8.8 billion tokens total across all languages.
4. Downstream Pretraining Validation:
   - IndicCorp has served as the canonical pretraining foundation for IndicBERT, IndicBART, Llama-3-Indic extensions,
     Gemma-Indic adaptations, and numerous state-of-the-art multilingual LLMs.
