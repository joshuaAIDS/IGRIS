This primary license covers all monolingual corpora partitions released under IndicCorp v1 and v2,
specifically including:
- Tamil (ta) [SRC-TM-01]
- Hindi (hi) [SRC-HI-01]
- Telugu (te) [SRC-OI-01]
- Malayalam (ml) [SRC-OI-02]
- Kannada (kn) [SRC-OI-03]
- Bengali (bn) [SRC-OI-04]
- Gujarati (gu) [SRC-OI-05]
